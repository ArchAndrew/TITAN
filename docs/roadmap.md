# TITAN Platform Roadmap

# Overview

TITAN follows an incremental product roadmap that evolves the platform through clearly defined releases.

Version 1 establishes the core Internal Developer Platform (IDP) capabilities, while future releases expand developer self-service, workflow orchestration, platform APIs, and AI-assisted operations.

Each release builds upon the previous platform foundation while maintaining enterprise security, governance, and operational excellence.

---

# Product Vision

The long-term vision for TITAN is to provide a fully governed Internal Developer Platform that enables engineering teams to provision, deploy, secure, observe, and operate cloud infrastructure through self-service workflows backed by automation, policy enforcement, and GitOps.

The roadmap prioritizes:

- Platform Engineering
- Developer Experience
- Cloud Governance
- DevSecOps
- Kubernetes Operations
- Observability
- Enterprise Automation

# Roadmap Philosophy

The roadmap reflects an incremental platform engineering strategy.

Each release expands platform capabilities while preserving the security, governance, operational consistency, and automation established in Version 1.

Rather than introducing isolated features, every enhancement is designed to strengthen TITAN as an enterprise Internal Developer Platform that scales with organizational growth.
